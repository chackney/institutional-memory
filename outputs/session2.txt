## How to get read-only prod access as a new hire (effective 2026-05-15)

**First: check your tenure.** You need to have been at the company for **at least 3 working days** (i.e., you can do this from day 4 onward). If you literally started today and tomorrow is day 2 or 3, you'll need to flag this to your manager — they may need to escalate.

**If you're eligible (day 4+), here are the steps:**

1. **Complete the "Prod Access Foundations" course** in the **BTS Learning portal**.
   - Self-paced, approximately 90 minutes.
   - Pass the end-of-course assessment.

2. **Get your manager's sign-off** on your certification.
   - This one-time approval is required before your first access request.
   - Loop your manager in immediately — do this in parallel with taking the course so they're ready to sign off as soon as you finish.

3. **Request access via the IAM platform.**
   - The completion email from the BTS Learning portal contains the direct link.
   - Submit your request there — not in Slack.

4. **Access is granted just-in-time**, scoped to a **4-hour window**.
   - If you need more time beyond 4 hours, simply re-request through the IAM platform.
   - Your manager will be notified of each request automatically (no additional approval needed after the initial certification).

---

**Who to talk to:**
- **Your direct manager** — you need their sign-off on your initial certification. This is the only human dependency.
- **No SRE contact needed** for read-only access.
- If you hit a technical problem with the BTS Learning portal or IAM platform, **Carlos Mendes** (Head of SRE) or **Maya Singh** (Head of Security) own this policy and are your escalation path.
