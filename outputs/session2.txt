## How to get read-only prod access as a new hire

### Step 1 — Check your tenure first
You must have been at the company for **at least 3 working days** before you can request access. If today is day 1 or 2, you cannot get standard access yet (see note below). You can request from **day 4** onward.

### Step 2 — Complete the certification (90 min, self-paced)
Go to the **BTS Learning portal** and complete the **"Prod Access Foundations"** course. It's self-paced and takes about 90 minutes. Pass the assessment at the end.

### Step 3 — Get manager sign-off on the certification
Your **direct manager** must sign off on your initial certification. Loop them in now so there's no delay — this is the one approval step in the whole process.

### Step 4 — Request access via the IAM platform
When you complete the course, you'll receive a **completion email with a link to the IAM platform**. Use that link to submit your access request. **Do not open a Slack ticket. Do not contact SRE directly.**

Access is granted **just-in-time**, scoped to a **4-hour window**. Your manager will be automatically notified of the request (no further approval needed from them for this individual request).

---

## People you need to talk to
- **Your direct manager** — for the initial certification sign-off. This is the only human approval required.
- No one else needs to be contacted. The process is self-serve through the IAM platform.

## Important caveat — tenure
If you literally just joined today and need access *tomorrow*, you may not yet meet the 3-working-day minimum. The policy is silent on exceptions to this rule for urgent cases. If you're blocked, I'd suggest raising the urgency with your manager — they can escalate to **Carlos Mendes** (Head of SRE, owns on-call rotation) or **Maya Singh** (Head of Security, co-owns this policy) to explore whether an exception path exists.
