## Your actual answer: How to get read-only prod access as a new joiner

Based on what I learned in our last session about the Production Access Policy (Source: `memory/policies.md` — "New read-only request process", effective 2026-05-15, owned by Maya Singh and Carlos Mendes):

### ⚠️ First: check your tenure

There is a **3 working-day minimum** before you can request access. You cannot get prod access until Day 4. If your issue is truly urgent and you're within your first 3 days, you'll need to escalate to your manager or the on-call SRE (Carlos Mendes owns the on-call rotation — check PagerDuty for who's on this week).

### Steps (assuming you're on Day 4 or later):

1. **Complete the "Prod Access Foundations" course** in the BTS Learning portal. It's 90 minutes, self-paced. Pass the end-of-course assessment.

2. **Check your completion email** — it will contain a direct link to the IAM platform access request form. Do not open a Slack ticket and do not DM anyone on the SRE team (that path no longer exists as of the current policy).

3. **Submit the IAM request.** Access is granted **just-in-time**, scoped to a **4-hour window**. If you need more time, simply re-request.

4. **Your manager will be auto-notified** of the request (but their sign-off is only required for your initial certification, not this individual request).
