## Getting Read-Only Prod Access

Here's exactly what you need to do:

### Step 1 — Check your eligibility
Read-only access normally requires **2 weeks of tenure** plus a completed pairing session with an SRE. If you just joined, you almost certainly don't meet the tenure requirement yet.

**However**, there is an exception that applies to your situation:

> If you need access urgently (e.g., to debug a P1 incident), the **on-call SRE can grant temporary 24-hour access immediately** — no pairing session required upfront. You'll then have 5 working days to complete the pairing session, or the access is automatically revoked.

(Source: access-policy.md — *Exception: urgent access requests*)
